# Source Synthetic Footage

## 1. A Dataset for Automatic Violence Detection in Videos

**Link:** https://github.com/airtlab/A-Dataset-for-Automatic-Violence-Detection-in-Videos

**Content:** Contains 350 MP4 clips, including 230 violent and 120 non-violent clips. The violent footage was staged by non-professional actors and includes simulated actions such as punching, kicking, slapping, clubbing, stabbing and gunshot scenarios.

**Licence:** Freely released for research and educational purposes. The authors request citation of the associated paper.

## 2. Smart-City CCTV Violence Detection Dataset (SCVD)

**Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/toluwaniaremu/smartcity-cctv-violence-detection-dataset-scvd

**Content:** Contains preprocessed CCTV-style video clips categorised into non-violent, violent and weaponised-violence scenarios.

**Licence:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Attribution is required, use must be non-commercial, and adaptations must be shared under the same licence.

## 3. Violence Detection – Combined

**Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/yash07yadav/project-data

**Content:** Contains separate classes for Fight, NonFight, HockeyFight and MovieFight.

**Licence:** MIT. The dataset combines footage from multiple existing datasets and sources, so underlying source permissions should also be considered before selecting individual clips.

## 4. Vision-based Fight Detection From Surveillance Cameras

**Link:** https://github.com/seymanurakti/fight-detection-surv-dataset

**Content:** Contains 300 two-second clips, including 150 fight and 150 non-fight clips. Fight scenes include kicking, hitting, wrestling and hitting with objects.
*Original links to the full YouTube videos are provided in the `video.txt` file.*
**Licence:** The repository is published under an MIT licence, and the authors request citation of the associated paper.
